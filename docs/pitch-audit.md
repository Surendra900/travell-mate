## 4. Slide-by-Slide Transformation Change Log

### Slide 1: Title & 10-Second Value Proposition
- **Old:** Generic title "TravelMate - National Startup Pitch 2026", vague tagline.
- **New:** High-contrast hero slide. Bold Cyan branding, clear 10-second value proposition, three capability badges (*Multimodal Split-Routing, Station Hopper Quota Hacks, Offline Boarding Pass & SOS*), presenter metadata, and personalized target: *Investment & Venture Team, YAI Infraventure*.

### Slide 2: The Problem
- **Old:** Slide 2 & 3 text walls with overlapping themes of waitlists and multi-app switching.
- **New:** Structured 3-card layout (*🔴 The Waitlist Dead-End, ⚠️ Manual Search Chaos, 🚨 Disconnection & Safety*). Active takeaway headline and key bottom insight bar.

### Slide 3: The Solution
- **Old:** Abstract explanation of multimodal travel without explaining mechanism.
- **New:** Three clear solution pillars (*1. AI Multimodal Routing, 2. Station Hopper Quota Hacks, 3. Offline Guardian*). Highlights legal IRCTC General Quota extensions and transfer safety buffers.

### Slide 4: How the Engine Works (The 3 Ranked Tiers)
- **Old:** Scattered throughout original slides 4, 5, 8.

  - *🟢 Paisa Vasool:* Sub-₹1,000 budget champion (Sleeper train + Bus).
  - *🔵 Smart Balanced:* Comfort & speed under ₹1,800 (Superfast AC train + AC sleeper bus).
